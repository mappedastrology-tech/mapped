# Mapped — Tier 2 Rituals (Light Spiritual / Crystal Starter Trio)

The "I bought my first crystals" tier. Built around the three most universally-recommended starter stones: **rose quartz, clear quartz, and amethyst.** These three together cover love, healing, clarity, manifestation, and protection — about 80% of what most users want from a ritual practice.

This tier also introduces basic incense and color-coded candles. Still grocery-store-and-Etsy accessible. No specialty metaphysical store required (though the experience is better if they go).

The starter trio costs roughly **$25–40 total** for tumbled stones from a reputable seller. That's the framing for users.

## RITUAL 26 — The Starter Trio Activation

**Category:** Foundation / Setup
**Best timing:** Day you receive your first crystals — do this before anything else
**Time needed:** 30 minutes
**Difficulty:** Beginner
**Tools needed:** Rose quartz, clear quartz, amethyst, water, salt

**You'll need:**
- 1 piece of rose quartz
- 1 piece of clear quartz
- 1 piece of amethyst
- A bowl of cool water
- A pinch of salt
- A clean cloth or towel
- A windowsill (ideally with moonlight access)

**The ritual:**
1. Add a pinch of salt to the bowl of water. Stir clockwise with your finger.
2. Submerge each crystal one at a time, holding for 10 seconds. (Note: this works for these three. Selenite and a few others dissolve in water — don't use this method for any future stones without checking first.)
3. Pat each one dry on the cloth.
4. Hold all three crystals cupped in both hands at heart level.
5. Speak aloud, slowly: *"I welcome you. I'm new at this. Teach me what I need to know."*
6. Address each one specifically:
   - To rose quartz: *"You are for love — for myself first, and for what comes after."*
   - To clear quartz: *"You are for clarity — show me what's true."*
   - To amethyst: *"You are for peace — quiet what doesn't need to be loud."*
7. Place all three on a windowsill where moonlight will hit them overnight (any moon phase works — you're charging, not full-moon-ing).
8. In the morning, retrieve them. They're ready.

**Affirmation to carry:** *"I am beginning. I'm allowed to not know everything yet."*

**After:** Keep your trio together when not in use — a small dish, a pouch, a corner of your nightstand. They become a "set" energetically the more you use them together. Repeat the saltwater cleanse monthly, or whenever a crystal feels "heavy" or "dull" in your hand.

## RITUAL 27 — Heart-Opening Practice

**Category:** Love / Self-love
**Best timing:** Morning, daily for 21 days
**Time needed:** 7 minutes
**Difficulty:** Beginner
**Tools needed:** Rose quartz

**You'll need:**
- 1 piece of rose quartz
- A quiet spot

**The ritual:**
1. Sit with the rose quartz in your dominant hand.
2. Place your other hand over your heart.
3. Take 7 slow breaths. With each inhale, imagine pink light pouring from the stone into your chest. With each exhale, soften.
4. Move the stone to rest directly over your heart. Cover it with your hand.
5. Speak aloud, three times: *"I am love. I receive love. I am love."*
6. Sit in silence for 3 minutes. If your mind wanders, just come back to the stone's weight against your chest.
7. End by kissing the stone (yes, really). Place it in your bra, pocket, or under your pillow for the day.

**Affirmation to carry:** *"My heart is open and protected at the same time."*

**After:** This is a 21-day practice for a reason. The first week feels routine. The second week, you'll start noticing when your guard goes up automatically. The third week, things shift. Stick with it.

## RITUAL 28 — Clear Quartz Intention Programming

**Category:** Manifestation
**Best timing:** New moon, or the start of any new project
**Time needed:** 15 minutes
**Difficulty:** Beginner
**Tools needed:** Clear quartz, paper, pen

**You'll need:**
- 1 piece of clear quartz (point or tumbled, both work)
- A small piece of paper
- A pen
- A small dish or pouch

**The ritual:**
1. Sit somewhere quiet. Hold the clear quartz between your palms.
2. On the paper, write **one specific intention** in present tense. Keep it to one sentence. *"I am earning $5,000 per month from my own work by [date]."*
3. Hold the paper in one hand, the crystal in the other.
4. Read the intention aloud, three times. Each time, feel the words actually land — don't just recite them.
5. Now: hold the crystal directly to your forehead (third eye). Breathe on it three times — slow, warm breath. You're "programming" the stone.
6. Speak: *"This crystal holds my intention. Every time I see it, my intention activates."*
7. Fold the paper. Place the crystal on top of the paper in a small dish, pouch, or visible spot on your altar/desk.
8. Each time you walk past it during the day, touch the crystal. That re-activates the intention.

**Affirmation to carry:** *"What I have programmed is becoming."*

**After:** Clear quartz is the "amplifier" stone — it will amplify whatever intention you program into it. Be careful what you program. If you change your mind on the intention, cleanse the stone (saltwater, sage smoke, or running water) and program it fresh.

## RITUAL 29 — Amethyst Sleep Ritual

**Category:** Sleep / Healing
**Best timing:** Nightly, especially during high-stress weeks
**Time needed:** 10 minutes (then sleep)
**Difficulty:** Beginner
**Tools needed:** Amethyst

**You'll need:**
- 1 piece of amethyst (small enough to comfortably fit under a pillow)
- Your bed

**The ritual:**
1. About 30 minutes before sleep, get into bed. Lights low.
2. Hold the amethyst on your forehead between your brows (third eye) for 2 minutes.
3. Slow your breathing — in for 4, hold for 4, out for 8. Repeat.
4. Mentally scan from your toes to your head, releasing tension as you go. Each body part: *"I release you for the night."*
5. Speak aloud: *"My mind rests. My body restores. I wake renewed."*
6. Place the amethyst under your pillow (not against your face — under, where you won't roll onto it).
7. Turn off the light. Don't pick your phone back up.

**Affirmation to carry (when waking):** *"I rested. I receive what today brings."*

**After:** Amethyst has been associated with peaceful sleep, prophetic dreams, and protection during dream-states for thousands of years. (Greek nobility wore it specifically for this.) Cleanse it weekly under cool running water or in moonlight — sleep crystals absorb a lot.

## RITUAL 30 — The Three-Stone Decision Ritual

**Category:** Clarity / Decision-making
**Best timing:** When you can't decide between options
**Time needed:** 20 minutes
**Difficulty:** Beginner
**Tools needed:** All three trio crystals

**You'll need:**
- Rose quartz, clear quartz, amethyst
- Paper and pen
- A flat surface

**The ritual:**
1. Sit with all three stones in front of you.
2. Write your decision at the top of the paper. Then write the options below — usually 2 or 3.
3. Pick up the **rose quartz** first. Hold it. Ask yourself, out loud: *"Which option does my heart want?"* Write the answer next to "heart:" without overthinking.
4. Set rose quartz down. Pick up **clear quartz**. *"Which option is clearest? Which one has fewer unknowns?"* Write under "mind:".
5. Set clear quartz down. Pick up **amethyst**. *"Which option will give me the most peace six months from now?"* Write under "spirit:".
6. Look at the three answers. Often they agree. When they don't — that's the actual conversation you need to have with yourself.
7. End by holding all three stones together. Speak: *"I trust what I know."*

**Affirmation to carry:** *"My heart, mind, and spirit are aligned in this decision."*

**After:** This works because you're forcing yourself to consider the same choice through three different lenses. The crystals are the focus tool. The lenses are the actual practice. Use this for jobs, relationships, big purchases, moves.

## RITUAL 31 — Crystal Bath Soak

**Category:** Cleansing / Self-love
**Best timing:** Sunday evenings, after hard weeks
**Time needed:** 30 minutes
**Difficulty:** Beginner
**Tools needed:** Bathtub, rose quartz, salt, optional candle

**You'll need:**
- A bathtub
- Rose quartz (and optionally amethyst — *not* clear quartz, some are unstable in heat over time)
- 1 cup salt (Epsom or sea)
- Optional: a candle, dried lavender or rose petals, soft music

**The ritual:**
1. Run a warm bath. Add salt under running water, stir clockwise with your hand.
2. Place rose quartz (and amethyst if using) directly in the water, one at each end of the tub.
3. Light a candle if using. Lights low.
4. Before getting in, kneel beside the tub. Place your hand on the water. Speak: *"This water holds love. This water holds peace. I receive both."*
5. Get in. Settle. Let yourself be quiet for at least 5 minutes before doing anything else.
6. At some point during the soak, hold the rose quartz directly over your heart. Stay there for at least 3 minutes.
7. Soak for at least 20 minutes total.
8. Before getting out, pull the drain plug while still in the water. Watch it lower around you. Pick up the crystals as the water leaves so they don't go down the drain.
9. Speak: *"What was washed off goes with this water. What I love stays with me."*

**Affirmation to carry:** *"I am someone worth this kind of care."*

**After:** Pat the crystals dry. They've been working — let them rest on a windowsill in moonlight overnight before using again. Drink water. Go to bed early.

## RITUAL 32 — The Pocket Practice

**Category:** Daily / Grounding
**Best timing:** Every morning, especially before hard days
**Time needed:** 2 minutes
**Difficulty:** Beginner
**Tools needed:** One trio crystal

**You'll need:**
- One of your three crystals (choose by what you need today)
- A pocket, bra, or bag

**The ritual:**
1. Each morning, pause before getting dressed.
2. Look at all three crystals. Ask yourself: *"What do I need today?"*
   - **Soft, scared, or tender?** → Rose quartz
   - **Foggy, indecisive, or scattered?** → Clear quartz
   - **Anxious, overwhelmed, or wired?** → Amethyst
3. Pick up the chosen stone. Hold it to your heart for 30 seconds.
4. Speak one sentence aloud about what you need from today: *"I need to be patient with myself." / "I need to make a clear choice." / "I need to stay calm in the meeting."*
5. Slip the crystal into your pocket, bra, or bag.
6. Throughout the day, when you remember it's there, touch it. That's the practice.

**Affirmation to carry:** *"I have what I need with me."*

**After:** This is the most "everyday" of all crystal practices. Most consistent crystal users do some version of this. The point isn't that the crystal does the work — the point is that touching it is a *prompt* to come back to your intention.

## RITUAL 33 — Incense Cleanse + Crystal Charge

**Category:** Maintenance / Cleansing
**Best timing:** Weekly, ideally Sunday
**Time needed:** 15 minutes
**Difficulty:** Beginner
**Tools needed:** Incense (any kind), all crystals, fire-safe holder

**You'll need:**
- An incense stick (sandalwood, frankincense, sage, palo santo, or any grocery-store incense)
- A fire-safe holder
- All your crystals (start with the trio, expand as you collect)
- A lighter

**The ritual:**
1. Open a window for ventilation.
2. Light the incense, blow out any flame, place in holder.
3. Lay your crystals out on a clean surface — a cloth, a tray, your altar if you have one.
4. One at a time, pick up each crystal and pass it through the smoke 3–5 times. As you do, speak aloud (or silently): *"Cleansed. Renewed. Ready."*
5. After all crystals are smoke-cleansed, hold your hands over the group. Speak: *"Thank you for the work you've done. Rest, then return."*
6. Leave the crystals out for at least 1 hour to "breathe" before putting them away.
7. Let the incense burn fully or extinguish in the holder.

**Affirmation to carry:** *"I tend to my tools. They tend to me."*

**After:** Crystals genuinely do feel different after cleansing — heavier becomes lighter, dull becomes alive. You'll notice the shift after a few cycles. This should become a small weekly ritual you look forward to, not a chore.

**Cultural note for the app to display:** White sage smudging is a closed Indigenous practice. For weekly cleansing, frankincense, sandalwood, palo santo (sustainably sourced), or rosemary are all excellent options without the cultural appropriation concern.

## RITUAL 34 — The Color Candle Ritual

**Category:** Manifestation (varies by intention)
**Best timing:** Match the candle color to the intention; do on relevant moon phase
**Time needed:** 20 minutes
**Difficulty:** Beginner
**Tools needed:** A colored candle, matching crystal, paper

**You'll need:**
- A candle in the appropriate color:
  - **Green** — abundance, wealth, growth (pair with clear quartz or any green stone if you have one)
  - **Pink** — love, friendship, gentleness (pair with rose quartz)
  - **Red** — courage, passion, energy (pair with clear quartz)
  - **White** — clarity, new beginnings, all-purpose (pair with any)
  - **Black** — protection, banishing, boundaries (pair with clear quartz)
  - **Purple** — spiritual growth, intuition, dreams (pair with amethyst)
  - **Yellow/gold** — confidence, success, joy (pair with clear quartz)
- A small piece of paper, pen
- The matching crystal from your collection
- A heat-safe surface

**The ritual:**
1. Sit in a quiet space. Hold the candle and the crystal together in your hands.
2. On the paper, write one sentence describing what you're calling in. Match the color: green candle → wealth intention, pink candle → love intention, etc.
3. Read the intention aloud three times.
4. Place the paper under the candle holder. Place the crystal directly beside the candle.
5. Light the candle. Speak: *"As this candle burns, my intention grows."*
6. Sit with the candle for at least 10 minutes. Watch the flame. Don't multitask.
7. Either burn down completely (small candle) or extinguish with: *"This work continues until I light you again."* Re-light each evening for 5–10 minutes until done.

**Affirmation to carry:** Re-read your written intention each morning until the candle finishes.

**After:** When the candle is fully burned, bury the paper in a houseplant or outside, keep the crystal on your altar/desk as the active reminder.

## RITUAL 35 — The Trio Grid

**Category:** Foundation / Manifestation
**Best timing:** New moon, or any time you want to "set" a long intention
**Time needed:** 25 minutes
**Difficulty:** Intermediate
**Tools needed:** All three crystals, paper, pen

**You'll need:**
- All three crystals (rose quartz, clear quartz, amethyst)
- A piece of paper
- A pen
- A small flat surface where they can stay undisturbed for at least 24 hours (a windowsill, the corner of a desk, a small altar)

**The ritual:**
1. On the paper, write one large intention. This is a 30-day intention, not a daily one. *"I have a fulfilling new job by [date]." / "My relationship with my partner is honest and warm." / "My health is restored."*
2. Place the paper flat on your chosen surface.
3. Place the **clear quartz at the top center** of the paper (the "amplifier" position).
4. Place **rose quartz at the bottom left** (heart anchor).
5. Place **amethyst at the bottom right** (clarity anchor).
6. The three stones form a triangle around the paper.
7. Sit in front of the grid. Hold your hands over it, palms down. Speak: *"This intention is held by these stones. As long as they are here, the work continues."*
8. Sit in stillness for 10 minutes. Visualize the triangle glowing.
9. Leave the grid in place for at least one full lunar cycle (ideally new moon to new moon).
10. Touch the grid daily — just a finger to each stone — and re-state the intention silently.

**Affirmation to carry:** *"My intention is held even when I'm not paying attention."*

**After:** At the end of the cycle, dismantle the grid. Burn the paper safely (or bury it). Cleanse the crystals. Notice what shifted. This is one of the most powerful practices on the entire list.

# NOTES FOR COWORK

When generating Tier 2 rituals, the rules are:

1. **Use only the starter trio** (rose quartz, clear quartz, amethyst) plus anything from Tier 1. If a ritual requires citrine, black tourmaline, or anything else — it's Tier 3.
2. **Keep crystal care in mind.** Some crystals don't go in water, sun, or salt. The starter trio is generally safe for all of these — but Cowork should never assume that's true for any future crystal without checking.
3. **Always pair a crystal with an action.** Holding a stone alone is not a ritual — holding a stone *while breathing in a specific pattern, while speaking specific words, while writing something down* is a ritual.
4. **Color magic is unlocked at this tier.** Reference the candle color chart for any intention-based ritual.
5. **Cleansing methods to mention:** moonlight, sunlight (NOT for amethyst or rose quartz long-term — they fade), running water, salt water, incense smoke, sound (singing bowl or bell), or burying in earth overnight.
6. **The trio works as a system.** Many Tier 2 rituals should use 2 or 3 of them together, not just one in isolation. This builds toward the user's eventual full collection.
