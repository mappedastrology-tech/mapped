# Mapped — Zodiac-Specific Rituals (One Per Sign)

These are the rituals that make Mapped *different* from every other crystal/ritual app. Each is calibrated to the sign's element, modality (cardinal/fixed/mutable), ruling planet, traditional crystals, and the *actual psychology* of the sign — its strengths and shadow tendencies.

The pitch in the app: **"This ritual is built for your specific energy, not a one-size-fits-all spiritual practice."**

Each ritual specifies the tier so users with the toggle on can still see what's available to them.

## ARIES (Mar 21 – Apr 19) — The Cool-Down

**Element:** Fire | **Modality:** Cardinal | **Ruler:** Mars
**The shadow Aries needs to address:** Burnout, impulsivity, picking fights you don't actually want
**The strength to channel:** Initiation, courage, raw forward momentum

**Ritual: "The Pause Before You Send"**
**Tier:** 0 (no tools needed)
**Best timing:** When you're about to react to something — a text, an email, a confrontation
**Time needed:** 4 minutes

**The ritual:**
1. The second you feel the urge to fire off a response, *stop.* Hands off the keyboard. Phone face down.
2. Stand up. Walk to a different room.
3. Set a timer for 4 minutes. **Do not reduce this.** Aries energy will tell you 90 seconds is enough. It's not.
4. While the timer runs, do something physical: pushups, jumping jacks, shake out your arms hard, run cold water on your wrists. Burn the heat off your body, not into the message.
5. When the timer ends, ask yourself out loud: *"In 6 months, will I be glad I sent what I was about to send?"*
6. Now go back. Send something — or don't. Either way, you chose, instead of reacted.

**Affirmation to carry:** *"My fire is mine to direct."*

**After:** Aries needs the *delay*, not the silencing. You're not suppressing the fire — you're aiming it. Mars rules action, not reaction. This is a daily practice during eclipses, retrogrades, or any high-stakes month.

## TAURUS (Apr 20 – May 20) — The Pleasure Anchor

**Element:** Earth | **Modality:** Fixed | **Ruler:** Venus
**The shadow Taurus needs to address:** Stuck-ness, over-comfort, refusing to move when something has clearly ended
**The strength to channel:** Embodiment, sensual presence, deep loyalty to what's worthy

**Ritual: "The Five-Sense Reset"**
**Tier:** 1 (a few household items)
**Best timing:** Sunday afternoon, or any time you feel disconnected from your body
**Time needed:** 20 minutes

**You'll need:**
- Something delicious to eat (a piece of chocolate, a perfect piece of fruit, a fancy cheese — small but real)
- A scented candle, perfume, or fresh flowers
- Music you love
- A soft blanket or robe
- Good lighting (low + warm)

**The ritual:**
1. Make the space beautiful first. Light, scent, sound, texture.
2. Sit. Don't lie down — Taurus already loves lying down. This one's about *active* pleasure.
3. Engage each sense for 2 minutes:
   - **Sight:** Look at one beautiful thing. A flower, the candle, your own hand.
   - **Sound:** Close your eyes. Just listen to the music.
   - **Touch:** Feel the blanket, your skin, the chair under you.
   - **Smell:** Inhale slowly. Three deep breaths.
   - **Taste:** Eat the thing. Slowly. One small bite at a time.
4. After all five, sit for 5 more minutes doing nothing at all. This is the "earned rest."
5. Speak aloud: *"My body is the place where my life happens. I will not abandon it."*

**Affirmation to carry:** *"Pleasure is not frivolous. It is how I stay rooted."*

**After:** Taurus rules the throat and the senses. When you starve your senses, you also starve your voice. Repeat weekly. Resist the pull to skip it because "you've already done this one."

## GEMINI (May 21 – Jun 20) — The Single-Tab Ritual

**Element:** Air | **Modality:** Mutable | **Ruler:** Mercury
**The shadow Gemini needs to address:** Mental scatter, half-finished projects, talking instead of doing
**The strength to channel:** Curiosity, communication, pattern recognition

**Ritual: "One Question, Held"**
**Tier:** 0 (no tools needed)
**Best timing:** Morning, especially when overwhelmed by options
**Time needed:** 15 minutes

**The ritual:**
1. Sit somewhere with no screens. Not even music.
2. Pick **one** question. Just one. The hardest part for you. *"Should I take the job?" / "Why am I avoiding her text?" / "What do I actually want from this year?"*
3. Write the question at the top of a page. Then close the notebook.
4. Set a timer for 10 minutes. Sit with **only** that question. When your mind races to other questions, gently bring it back. *"Not now. We're with this one."*
5. After 10 minutes, open the notebook. Write whatever came up — even if it feels like nothing. Especially if it feels like nothing.
6. End by speaking aloud: *"I gave one thing my full attention. That was enough."*

**Affirmation to carry:** *"Depth is a place I'm allowed to live."*

**After:** Gemini's gift is breadth. The growth edge is depth. This isn't about silencing your curiosity — it's about giving one curiosity at a time the seriousness it deserves. Repeat anytime you have decision fatigue.

## CANCER (Jun 21 – Jul 22) — The Container Practice

**Element:** Water | **Modality:** Cardinal | **Ruler:** Moon
**The shadow Cancer needs to address:** Absorbing other people's feelings as your own, retreating into the shell when you actually need to speak
**The strength to channel:** Emotional intelligence, intuition, the gift of making people feel safe

**Ritual: "Whose Feeling Is This?"**
**Tier:** 1 (water + journal)
**Best timing:** End of day, especially after being around a lot of people
**Time needed:** 15 minutes

**You'll need:**
- A glass of water
- A journal
- Optional: any blue or silver-toned object (a piece of jewelry, a coin, a small bowl of water)

**The ritual:**
1. Sit somewhere quiet. Hold the glass of water in both hands.
2. Take 5 slow breaths. Feel the weight of the water — it's literally a small ocean in your hands.
3. Now mentally scan: what are you feeling right now? Really feeling. Sad? Anxious? Resentful? Sad-but-also-warm?
4. Ask yourself, out loud: *"Whose feeling is this?"*
5. Wait. Cancer absorbs energy from everyone around them, often without realizing. The answer might be: "Mine." Or it might be: "My mom's." Or: "That coworker who was crying earlier."
6. If it's not yours: drink half the water slowly. As you do, mentally release that feeling back to the person it belongs to (with love). Speak: *"I send this back to where it came from. With kindness, but not with me."*
7. If it is yours: write about it for 10 minutes. No editing. Just let it move.
8. End by pouring the rest of the water into a houseplant, or onto soil outside.

**Affirmation to carry:** *"I can feel deeply without taking on what isn't mine."*

**After:** Cancer rules the moon, which rules water. Water is your symbol and your medicine. Repeat every night during the full moon cycle if you've been emotionally drained.

## LEO (Jul 23 – Aug 22) — The Mirror of Worth

**Element:** Fire | **Modality:** Fixed | **Ruler:** Sun
**The shadow Leo needs to address:** Performing for love instead of receiving it; being seen but not really known
**The strength to channel:** Radiance, generosity, the courage to take up space without apology

**Ritual: "Mirror Without an Audience"**
**Tier:** 1 (a mirror + candle)
**Best timing:** Sunday evening, weekly
**Time needed:** 20 minutes

**You'll need:**
- A mirror you can sit in front of
- A gold or yellow candle (or any candle)
- A piece of paper, pen
- Privacy. Real privacy. Not "phone face down" — phone in another room.

**The ritual:**
1. Sit in front of the mirror. Light the candle. Place it next to you so it lights your face softly.
2. Look at yourself. Not at how you look — at *you.* Your eyes. Make eye contact for one full minute, no breaks.
3. Speak aloud: *"I see you when nobody is watching. I love you when nobody is clapping."*
4. On the paper, write three things you've done in the past week that no one knows about. Small, specific things. *"I let the elderly guy go ahead of me at CVS." / "I didn't snap at my brother when he deserved it." / "I went to bed early instead of pushing through."*
5. Read those three things aloud, looking at yourself in the mirror.
6. Speak: *"That was for me. I am someone I'm proud of, even when it's quiet."*
7. Blow out the candle. Touch your own cheek. Bow slightly to your reflection.

**Affirmation to carry:** *"I am loved even in the dark, even when no one is watching, even when I do nothing impressive."*

**After:** Leo's deepest fear is being unloved when not performing. This practice slowly retrains your nervous system to know — *you are still you* when the lights go down. Do it weekly until you no longer dread it.

## VIRGO (Aug 23 – Sep 22) — The Imperfection Offering

**Element:** Earth | **Modality:** Mutable | **Ruler:** Mercury
**The shadow Virgo needs to address:** Perfectionism, over-criticism (of self mostly), service that turns into self-erasure
**The strength to channel:** Discernment, devotion, the kind of care that actually fixes things

**Ritual: "The Done-Not-Perfect Offering"**
**Tier:** 1 (paper, candle, water)
**Best timing:** Sunday night, weekly
**Time needed:** 20 minutes

**You'll need:**
- A piece of paper
- A pen
- A candle (any kind)
- A glass of water

**The ritual:**
1. Light the candle. Sit at a clean surface.
2. On the paper, write a heading: **"What I did this week, imperfectly."**
3. List 5–10 things you started, attempted, or did less than perfectly this week. Don't list what you finished or did well — list the *messy* attempts. The text you sent that wasn't quite right. The workout you cut short. The dinner that came out fine but not great. The conversation you fumbled.
4. Read the list aloud, slowly.
5. After each one, speak: *"This was enough. This was already enough."*
6. When you finish: tear the list in half, but don't destroy it. Place one half under the candle. Drink half the water.
7. The other half of the list — the half you keep — represents the part of you that *did* show up, imperfectly, on purpose. Tape it somewhere only you will see (inside a journal, in a sock drawer, on the bathroom mirror).

**Affirmation to carry:** *"I am allowed to be in progress. The work is not the ladder — the work is the climbing."*

**After:** Virgo is the sign of the harvest, but also of the harvest *becoming useful*. Half of what you grow is for the pile, half is for the table. You don't have to perfect everything to be of service. Repeat weekly until your inner critic gets quieter.

## LIBRA (Sep 23 – Oct 22) — The Choosing Practice

**Element:** Air | **Modality:** Cardinal | **Ruler:** Venus
**The shadow Libra needs to address:** Indecision, people-pleasing, choosing harmony over honesty
**The strength to channel:** Diplomacy, beauty, the gift of seeing all sides

**Ritual: "The 30-Second Decision"**
**Tier:** 0 (no tools needed)
**Best timing:** Daily, for any decision under "medium" stakes
**Time needed:** 30 seconds (yes, really)

**The ritual:**
1. Throughout the day, when you face a small decision — what to eat, what to wear, whether to text back, whether to go to the thing — set a timer.
2. **30 seconds.** That's the entire window.
3. In those 30 seconds, ask yourself only this: *"What do I want?"* Not what's polite. Not what would please everyone. Not what's most balanced. *What do I want?*
4. When the timer goes off, commit to whatever your gut said. Even if it's not the "best" answer. Even if you might be wrong.
5. After committing, speak silently or aloud: *"I chose. I am allowed to want what I want."*
6. If you find yourself spiraling on the choice afterward, redirect: *"It's already chosen. I'm done."*

**Affirmation to carry:** *"My preferences matter, even when they inconvenience someone else."*

**After:** Libra's growth edge is *being a person*, not a referee between everyone else's preferences. Practice this on small things until it doesn't terrify you. Then practice it on bigger things. Venus rules Libra — Venus is also the planet of *desire*. You're allowed to have it.

## SCORPIO (Oct 23 – Nov 21) — The Shadow Tending

**Element:** Water | **Modality:** Fixed | **Ruler:** Pluto (modern), Mars (traditional)
**The shadow Scorpio needs to address:** Holding onto resentment, controlling through silence, refusing to let things die when they need to
**The strength to channel:** Depth, transformation, the gift of seeing what others won't look at

**Ritual: "What I Won't Bury"**
**Tier:** 1 (paper, candle, fire-safe bowl, water)
**Best timing:** New moon or dark moon
**Time needed:** 30 minutes

**You'll need:**
- Two pieces of paper
- A pen
- A black or dark candle (any candle works)
- A fire-safe bowl
- A glass of water

**The ritual:**
1. Light the candle. Sit somewhere with no interruptions for at least 30 minutes.
2. On the **first paper**, write: **"What I am ready to bury."** List the resentments, betrayals, fears, old versions of yourself you're done carrying. Don't be polite. This is private.
3. On the **second paper**, write: **"What I will not bury — and that's okay."** List the things you're keeping. The wounds that taught you. The trust you no longer give freely. The standards you've raised. *Some things shouldn't be released. Some things are meant to be kept and integrated.*
4. Read both lists aloud — first one, then the other.
5. Burn the first list safely in the bowl. Watch every word turn to ash. Speak: *"I bury you with respect. You served me. Now go."*
6. Drink the water — slowly. As you drink, fold the second list and place it somewhere private (a journal, a drawer). This list is *yours*. You don't have to forgive everything. You don't have to release everything. You don't have to "rise above."
7. End: *"I keep what's mine. I release what isn't. I am the one who decides."*

**Affirmation to carry:** *"My power is in knowing the difference between what to keep and what to let die."*

**After:** This ritual is built specifically for Scorpios because most "release rituals" tell you to let everything go. That's not your assignment. Your assignment is *discernment* — what dies, what transforms, what stays. Repeat at every new moon if needed.

## SAGITTARIUS (Nov 22 – Dec 21) — The Land Where You Are

**Element:** Fire | **Modality:** Mutable | **Ruler:** Jupiter
**The shadow Sagittarius needs to address:** Always looking for the next thing, refusing to land, wisdom without depth
**The strength to channel:** Vision, expansion, the gift of teaching what you've learned

**Ritual: "The Place I'm Already Standing"**
**Tier:** 0 (no tools needed)
**Best timing:** Whenever you start daydreaming about leaving — your job, your city, your relationship, your routine
**Time needed:** 15 minutes

**The ritual:**
1. Stop. Wherever you are. Sit down — even if you're at work, even if you're outside.
2. Plant your feet flat on the ground. Press them down hard into the floor or earth.
3. Take 5 slow breaths.
4. Look around your immediate space — within arm's reach. Name **5 things you genuinely love about this exact moment**. Not the future, not somewhere else. Here. Now. The light through that window. The texture of this fabric. The quiet right now.
5. Speak aloud: *"This is the place I am standing. I will not abandon it for somewhere I haven't even gotten to yet."*
6. Now ask yourself: *"What's one small thing I can deepen instead of expand?"* A friendship I already have. A skill I'm halfway through. A book I started. A practice I've abandoned three times.
7. Commit to that thing for the next 7 days. Don't add anything new. Just go deeper into what's already in front of you.

**Affirmation to carry:** *"The horizon is always there. It will wait for me. I can be here for now."*

**After:** Sag's gift is vision — the long view, the next continent, the bigger meaning. Your growth edge is letting one thing fully form before you sprint toward the next one. Jupiter expands what it touches. Slow down enough for the expansion to actually take root.

## CAPRICORN (Dec 22 – Jan 19) — The Permission to Rest

**Element:** Earth | **Modality:** Cardinal | **Ruler:** Saturn
**The shadow Capricorn needs to address:** Earning love through achievement, rest as failure, isolation as discipline
**The strength to channel:** Mastery, integrity, the long game

**Ritual: "The Productive Nothing"**
**Tier:** 0 (no tools needed)
**Best timing:** Sunday afternoon, weekly. Non-negotiable.
**Time needed:** 60 minutes (yes, an hour)

**The ritual:**
1. Block one hour on your calendar. Write "REST" on it. Treat it like a meeting with your most important client. (For you, this might actually work — frame rest as a deliverable.)
2. When the hour starts: phone in another room. Laptop closed. No podcast, no audiobook, no "productive" rest.
3. Sit or lie somewhere comfortable. Stare at a wall. Watch a tree out the window. Pet a dog.
4. The rule: **do nothing that has an outcome.** No emails. No errands. No "I'll just quickly..." No.
5. If you feel guilty (you will), speak aloud: *"This rest is the work. The work is not done without this rest."*
6. When the hour ends, write down ONE thing you noticed during the rest. Just one. *(A thought you'd been avoiding, a feeling you didn't realize you had, a memory, a preference, an idea.)*
7. That one thing is what the rest was *for.* Capricorn doesn't rest randomly — Capricorn rests to *receive*.

**Affirmation to carry:** *"Rest is not failure. Rest is what allows the climb to continue."*

**After:** Saturn rules Capricorn. Saturn is the planet of structure — but also of *limits*. You are not a machine. The mountain doesn't get climbed faster by the goat that doesn't sleep. Repeat weekly. Forever.

## AQUARIUS (Jan 20 – Feb 18) — The Embodiment Drop

**Element:** Air | **Modality:** Fixed | **Ruler:** Saturn (traditional), Uranus (modern)
**The shadow Aquarius needs to address:** Emotional detachment, intellectualizing feelings, alone-as-superior
**The strength to channel:** Vision, individuality, the gift of seeing systems no one else can

**Ritual: "From the Neck Down"**
**Tier:** 0 or 1 (water optional)
**Best timing:** End of day, especially after intense thinking
**Time needed:** 20 minutes

**You'll need:**
- A space where you can lie down
- Optional: a bath (Tier 1)

**The ritual:**
1. Lie flat on your back. Hands at your sides. Eyes closed.
2. Take 5 deep breaths into your belly. Not your chest — your belly. Most Aquariuses breathe shallowly into the chest because all the action is happening in the head.
3. Now: scan your body, slowly, from feet to head. At each body part, ask: *"What am I feeling here?"*
4. Don't think about the answer. **Feel it.** Tightness in the calves. Heat in the thighs. Tension in the jaw. Hollow in the chest.
5. When you find a sensation, stay with it for 30 seconds before moving on. Don't analyze it. Don't ask what it "means." Just feel it.
6. At the end of the scan, place one hand on your heart and one on your belly. Speak aloud: *"I am not just my mind. I am also a body. I am also a feeling. I'm allowed to live below my neck."*
7. (Tier 1 upgrade: do this in a warm bath instead of on the floor. Water makes the body impossible to ignore.)

**Affirmation to carry:** *"My feelings are data, not weakness."*

**After:** Aquarius's gift is the long view, the system, the future. The growth edge is the body — which is the *only* place feelings actually live. Repeat anytime you've been "in your head" for too long.

## PISCES (Feb 19 – Mar 20) — The Boundary Bath

**Element:** Water | **Modality:** Mutable | **Ruler:** Jupiter (traditional), Neptune (modern)
**The shadow Pisces needs to address:** Dissolution into other people, escape through fantasy, feeling everything without filtering anything
**The strength to channel:** Imagination, empathy, the gift of seeing the unseen

**Ritual: "The Container of Self"**
**Tier:** 1 (water + salt)
**Best timing:** Whenever you've lost yourself — in a relationship, in a project, in someone else's bad mood
**Time needed:** 30 minutes

**You'll need:**
- A bathtub OR a basin of water for your feet
- 1/2 cup of salt (sea salt or Epsom)
- A small towel
- Optional: a candle, lavender oil

**The ritual:**
1. Run a warm bath (or fill a basin with warm water if no tub).
2. Pour the salt in. Stir it with your hand. Speak: *"This salt holds me in. This water knows where I end and where the world begins."*
3. Get in. Submerge as much of you as possible, or soak your feet.
4. Close your eyes. Now mentally trace the outline of your body — *literally*. Start at the top of your head. Slowly trace: your forehead, your cheeks, your jaw, your shoulders, down your arms, around your hands, back up, across your chest, down your sides, around your feet, back up the other side, back to the top of your head.
5. As you trace, speak: *"This is where I end. This is where I begin. Inside this line is mine. Outside is not."*
6. Sit in the water for at least 15 minutes. If your mind drifts into someone else's life, gently return: *"That's not mine to carry."*
7. Pull the drain (or pour out the basin) while still in the water. Speak: *"What isn't mine goes with the water."* Stay until the water is gone.
8. Pat dry — slowly. Notice the towel against each part of your body. You are a body. With edges. With a shape that isn't shared with anyone.

**Affirmation to carry:** *"I have edges. They are sacred. They are mine."*

**After:** Pisces's gift is dissolving into something larger than you — the ocean, the spirit, the collective. The growth edge is *coming back* into yourself afterward. Without that return, the gift becomes a drowning. Repeat anytime you feel "swallowed" by someone else's reality.

# THE CROSS-SIGN BONUS RITUAL

For when a user wants something that pulls in their **whole chart**, not just their sun:

## RITUAL: "The Big Three Check-In"

**Tier:** 0 + paper
**Best timing:** Once a season (every 3 months)
**Time needed:** 30 minutes

**The ritual:**
1. Open Mapped (or wherever the user has their chart). Note your **sun, moon, and rising signs**.
2. On a piece of paper, divide into three columns: SUN / MOON / RISING.
3. Under SUN, write: *"What is my external life asking for right now?"* Answer in 1–3 sentences.
4. Under MOON, write: *"What is my emotional self asking for right now?"* Answer.
5. Under RISING, write: *"What is the world meeting first when it meets me right now?"* Answer.
6. Look at all three. Are they aligned? Or are they pulling in different directions?
7. Pick the **one** that feels most neglected. That's your sign for the next 30 days.
8. Look up the ritual for that specific sign in the app. Do it weekly for the month.

**Affirmation to carry:** *"I am made of layers. Each one matters."*

**After:** This is the kind of ritual only an astrology app can offer. It's also a way to keep users coming back to the app, since the answer changes every 3 months as transits shift their priorities.

# NOTES FOR COWORK

When generating sign-specific rituals, the rules are:

1. **Lead with the shadow, not the strength.** Most astrology apps just hype each sign's strengths. Mapped's edge is being honest about each sign's growth edge. Aries readers know they're impulsive. Capricorns know they over-work. Honor that with rituals built for the actual *growth*, not just the flattery.

2. **Match the ritual to the element:**
   - **Fire signs** (Aries, Leo, Sag): rituals about *cooling, slowing, witnessing yourself*
   - **Earth signs** (Taurus, Virgo, Cap): rituals about *flexibility, rest, imperfection*
   - **Air signs** (Gemini, Libra, Aquarius): rituals about *embodiment, depth, decision*
   - **Water signs** (Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces): rituals about *boundaries, containment, discernment*

3. **Reference the ruler.** Saturn rules Capricorn → discipline + limits. Mars rules Aries → action + restraint. Venus rules Taurus and Libra → pleasure + choice. This makes the ritual feel astrologically literate, not just zodiac-flavored.

4. **The shadow ritual should feel uncomfortable to the sign.** If a Leo's ritual is comfortable, it's not the right one. The growth edge always feels a little bad before it feels freeing.

5. **Tier the rituals.** At least one Tier 0 ritual per sign so even tool-free users get the personalization. Then build Tier 1+ versions for users who want to expand.

6. **Don't roast the sign.** There's a fine line between "honest about the shadow" and "ragging on the sign." The tone should feel like a wise friend who knows you well — not like an astrology meme account.
