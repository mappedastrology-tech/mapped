# Mapped — Content Marketing & SEO Strategy

**Version 1.0 · July 2026 · Owner: Taylor**
**Companion to:** `Mapped_Positioning_Messaging_Strategy.md` (voice, pillars, audience)

This doc is the plan for how Mapped earns organic attention — search, App Store, social, and
email — and turns it into installs. It names the keywords worth ranking for, the pages to
build, and the cadence to publish on.

> **On the numbers below.** Search volumes and difficulty here are **directional estimates**
> from category/SERP research, not exact Semrush pulls. They're accurate enough to prioritize.
> Before committing a quarter of work, validate the top 20 targets in a keyword tool. Every
> volume is labeled US-monthly and bucketed (🔴 very high / 🟠 high / 🟡 medium / 🟢 long-tail)
> rather than given false precision.

---

## 1. TL;DR — the five moves

1. **Build free tool pages ("money pages").** The highest-intent, highest-volume astrology
   searches are tools — *birth chart calculator*, *free natal chart*, *moon phase today*,
   *compatibility/synastry*. The sites winning this category (astro-seek, cafeastrology,
   astrology.com) are tools, and CHANI built `chani.com/chart` to grab a slice. **A free,
   beautiful birth chart calculator on the Mapped site is the single biggest lever we have.**
   It ranks, it demonstrates the product, and it funnels straight into signup.

2. **Own a topic cluster, not scattered posts.** Pick pillars (Birth Chart, Transits & Timing,
   Rituals, Tarot, Learn-Astrology) and build deep hub-and-spoke clusters. Depth + internal
   linking is how a new domain beats thin competitors.

3. **Programmatic SEO for the long tail.** Sign pages, sign-pairing compatibility pages, "X in
   Y house" pages, and transit pages are templatable and number in the thousands. This is where
   a content-rich app like Mapped can out-publish everyone.

4. **Win the skeptic keyword no one else targets.** "Is astrology real," "astrology for
   skeptics," "grounded/psychological astrology" — low competition, perfectly on-brand, and it
   feeds our biggest untapped segment (the Curious Skeptic).

5. **ASO is SEO's twin.** Half of discovery is in the App Store. The keyword field, subtitle,
   and description (see positioning doc §8.3) should target the same head terms.

## 2. The SEO opportunity thesis

**Where the traffic is.** Astrology search splits into three intents:

- **Tool/transactional** ("birth chart calculator," "free natal chart," "moon phase today") —
  highest volume, highest install intent. People want to *do* something. Dominated by old,
  ugly, ad-heavy tool sites. **This is the beachhead.**
- **Informational** ("what is a rising sign," "Saturn return meaning," "how to read a birth
  chart") — enormous long-tail, medium intent, great for authority and internal linking.
- **Identity/entertainment** ("Scorpio traits," "Gemini compatibility," "today's horoscope") —
  massive volume, lower intent, best served programmatically and on social.

**Why Mapped can win despite being new:** the incumbents are either (a) tool sites with no
brand, no app, and dated UX (astro-seek, cafeastrology), or (b) apps with thin websites
(Co-Star's site is basically a landing page; CHANI has a few tool pages). Nobody is combining a
*beautiful free tool + genuine editorial depth + an app to convert into*. That's the gap.

**The brand advantage in content:** our voice (a lens, not a prediction; grounded → mystical)
lets us write the one thing this category lacks — content a skeptic will actually read and
share. That's a linkable, differentiated angle competitors literally can't copy without
betraying their own tone.

## 4. The pillar–cluster content model

Structure every URL into a **hub (pillar) → spoke (cluster)** map. Pillars are broad,
authoritative guides; spokes are specific pages that link up to the pillar and across to the
tool. This is how a new domain builds topical authority fast.

```
/birth-chart  (PILLAR: "How to read your birth chart")
   ├── /tools/birth-chart-calculator      ← money page
   ├── /rising-sign
   ├── /big-three
   ├── /sun-in-[sign] × 12
   ├── /moon-in-[sign] × 12
   └── /[planet]-in-[house] × programmatic

/transits  (PILLAR: "Astrology of timing")
   ├── /tools/moon-phase-today             ← money page
   ├── /saturn-return
   ├── /mercury-retrograde
   └── /eclipses-2026

/compatibility  (PILLAR: "Astrological compatibility")
   ├── /tools/synastry-calculator          ← money page
   └── /[sign]-and-[sign] × 144            ← programmatic

/tarot, /numerology, /human-design, /crystals  (each: pillar + free tool + spokes)

/grounded  (PILLAR: "Astrology for skeptics")   ← the wedge, brand-defining
```

**Rule:** every spoke links up to its pillar and sideways to the relevant free tool; every tool
has a persistent "Get the full read in the app" CTA. Every page carries the brand voice — *a
lens, not a prediction* — so content and product feel like one world.

## 5. Money pages (build these first)

Free, genuinely useful, gorgeous tools that rank on transactional terms and convert to installs.
Priority order:

1. **Birth Chart Calculator** — target: *birth chart calculator, free natal chart*. Enter
   birth data → render a beautiful wheel + big-three summary → "See your full chart, transits,
   and daily reads in the app." This is the flagship. It also doubles as a live demo.
2. **Moon Phase Today** — target: *moon phase today, full/new moon*. Live phase + one-line
   grounded meaning + a tiny ritual → app CTA. Evergreen, high repeat traffic.
3. **Compatibility / Synastry Calculator** — target: *zodiac compatibility, synastry*. Two
   charts → a "how you line up" readout → Maps feature CTA.
4. **Numerology (Life Path) Calculator** and **Human Design Chart** — each captures its own
   tool query and showcases breadth.

**Conversion pattern for every tool page:** deliver real value for free first, then offer the
depth only the app has (daily reads, transits, saved charts, Dolly, rituals). Never gate the
core result — that's the anti-manipulation promise, and it's also what earns the ranking.

## 6. Programmatic SEO (the scale play)

Templatable page sets, each generated from the app's existing content/data:

- **12 sign pages** + 24 sign man/woman → identity volume.
- **144 sign-pairing compatibility pages** ("Aries and Leo compatibility").
- **~120 "planet in sign"** and **~120 "planet in house"** pages (you already have Library
  reference entries — repurpose them as indexable pages).
- **78 tarot card pages** (again, Library content made public).
- **Transit pages** updated on a schedule (Almanac data → public pages).

**Guardrails:** each page must be genuinely differentiated (pull from your real interpretive
content, not spun text), carry a unique intro, and internally link. Thin doorway pages get
penalized; deep, honest ones win. Roll out in batches of ~50 and watch indexation before
scaling. This is where Mapped's content depth becomes an unfair advantage.

## 7. Editorial / blog cadence

Beyond tools and programmatic pages, a lean editorial engine builds authority and gives social
and email something to point at.

**Format mix (aim ~2–4 pieces/week at launch, sustainable over volume):**
- **Evergreen pillar guides** (the backbone — Saturn return, how to read a chart, etc.).
- **Seasonal/timely** (this month's sky, eclipse seasons, Mercury retrograde windows, full-moon
  ritual of the month) — recurring, calendar-driven, great for email + social.
- **The skeptic essays** (flagship, linkable — "Why astrology works even if it isn't real").
- **Ritual of the week** — pull directly from your ritual system (tier/tone), a natural content
  engine you already have built.

**Repurposing loop:** one pillar guide → a carousel/TikTok script → an email → 3–4 programmatic
spokes. Write once, atomize everywhere.

## 8. Channel strategy

**Organic Search (primary):** tools + pillars + programmatic, per above. Long game, compounding.

**ASO (co-primary):** the App Store is search too. Target the head terms (*astrology, birth
chart, horoscope, tarot, natal chart, moon*) in the app name, subtitle, and keyword field. Use
the positioning doc §8.3 listing. Iterate the subtitle and screenshots as A/B tests.

**Social — TikTok / Instagram / Pinterest (top-of-funnel):** this is where astrology *spreads*.
Short, specific, grounded takes ("your Saturn return is a renovation, not a curse"), the
skeptic angle, and ritual clips. Pinterest is underrated for evergreen tool/guide traffic.
Every piece points to a free tool page, not straight to the App Store — the tool converts
better.

**Email (retention + re-engagement):** capture emails on the tool pages ("get your full chart
reading"), then a grounded weekly "sky note." Calm cadence, on-brand — the anti-doom
newsletter.

**Digital PR / backlinks (authority):** the skeptic essays and original angles ("we surveyed
X," "the psychology of why astrology feels accurate") are pitchable to lifestyle/wellness
press. Backlinks to the pillar pages are what make the whole domain rank.

## 10. Measurement — what to watch

**Leading (weekly):** pages indexed, keywords ranking top 100 / top 10, tool-page sessions,
tool → signup conversion rate.
**Core (monthly):** non-brand organic sessions, App Store impressions → product page views →
installs, keyword rankings for the priority 20, email captures from tools.
**Lagging (quarterly):** organic install share, blended CAC vs. paid, domain authority/backlinks.

**North-star for content:** *organic installs per month*. The birth-chart calculator's
signup-conversion rate is the single metric that tells you if the beachhead is working.

## 12. Guardrails — content is still Mapped

Every published word inherits the positioning doc's rules. Specifically:

- **A lens, not a prediction.** SEO tempts you toward "what will happen to Scorpio in 2026."
  Reframe to understanding, not fortune-telling. It's better content *and* on-brand.
- **No fear-baiting for clicks.** No "the transit that will ruin your week." Calm, specific,
  useful.
- **Free means free.** Tool pages deliver a real result before any app CTA. That honesty is
  both the ethic and the ranking strategy.
- **Grounded is a feature, not a hedge.** Write the skeptic content with conviction — it's our
  most defensible, most linkable territory.
- Run all copy through the **Copy Audit Rubric** (positioning doc §10) before publishing.
