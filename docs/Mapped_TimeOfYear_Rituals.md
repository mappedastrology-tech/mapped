# THE FOUR SOLAR THRESHOLDS

These are the four "tentpoles" of the year — the equinoxes and solstices. Almost every spiritual tradition has rituals tied to these dates because they're astronomically real shifts in the planet's relationship to the sun.

## SPRING EQUINOX (~March 20–21) — The Beginning

**What it is:** The sun crosses into Aries. Day and night are equal length. From here, the days get longer for 6 months. New zodiac year. Fresh start.

**The energy:** Initiation, new growth, planting, courage to begin.

**Ritual: "The First Seed"**
**Tier:** 1 (a seed/bulb + soil + paper)
**Time needed:** 30 minutes

**You'll need:**
- A small pot of soil OR a houseplant you already own
- A seed (anything — even a kitchen seed: lemon, apple, sunflower) OR a bulb
- A piece of paper
- A pen
- A cup of water

**The ritual:**
1. Sit with the empty pot or plant in front of you. Hold the seed in your hand.
2. On the paper, write **one specific intention** for the next 6 months. From now until the autumn equinox.
3. Read the intention aloud. Three times.
4. Tear the paper into small pieces. Mix the pieces directly into the soil.
5. Plant the seed in the soil — over the buried paper. (Or, if working with an existing plant, bury the paper and seed at the base of the roots.)
6. Pour the water in slowly. As you do, speak: *"What I plant today, I tend until autumn. I trust the slow work."*
7. Place the pot somewhere you'll see it daily.

**Affirmation to carry:** *"I am at the beginning. The beginning has its own gifts."*

**After:** Tend the plant. Water it. Notice when it grows. At the autumn equinox (~Sept 22), check in with the original intention — what's grown, what hasn't, what's surprised you.

## SUMMER SOLSTICE (~June 20–21) — The Peak

**What it is:** The sun crosses into Cancer. Longest day of the year. Maximum light.

**The energy:** Fullness, peak power, gratitude, claiming your light.

**Ritual: "Standing in Your Own Light"**
**Tier:** 0 (no tools needed)
**Time needed:** 20 minutes (do this outside if at all possible)

**The ritual:**
1. Go outside. Anywhere — your yard, a park, a balcony, the sidewalk.
2. Find a spot in direct sun. Stand. Take off your shoes if it's safe.
3. Tilt your face toward the sun (eyes closed). Open your arms wide.
4. Breathe deeply for 7 breaths. With each inhale, feel the sun pouring into your chest.
5. Speak aloud — and you might need to do this somewhere private if you're not loud-in-public type: *"I am here. I am whole. I have grown more than I realize."*
6. Now name **three things you've already accomplished this year**. Not what you're still working on — what's *done*. (Capricorn rising types will struggle here. That's the point.)
7. After each one, speak: *"I see this. I honor this. This was real work."*
8. End by placing both hands over your heart. Speak: *"I claim this light as mine. The work I've done is enough to celebrate now."*

**Affirmation to carry:** *"I do not have to wait until I'm 'finished' to celebrate."*

**After:** This is the hardest ritual on this list for most people. We're trained to focus on what isn't done. The summer solstice is a forced pause to honor what *is.*

## AUTUMN EQUINOX (~September 22–23) — The Harvest

**What it is:** The sun crosses into Libra. Day and night equal again. From here, the nights get longer.

**The energy:** Reaping, gratitude, balance, beginning the descent inward.

**Ritual: "The Two Lists"**
**Tier:** 1 (paper + candle + something you grew/made/earned this year)
**Time needed:** 30 minutes

**You'll need:**
- A piece of paper
- A pen
- A candle
- One small physical item that represents something you "harvested" this year (a paycheck stub, a thank-you card, a photo, a ring, a plant you grew, a book you finished, anything that's evidence of growth)

**The ritual:**
1. Light the candle. Sit with the harvest item in front of you.
2. On the paper, write two columns: **HARVESTED** and **STILL GROWING**.
3. Under HARVESTED, list everything that *did* come to fruition this year. Big and small. The friendship that deepened. The skill you actually learned. The pound you lost (or gained, if that was the goal). Be honest. Don't be modest.
4. Under STILL GROWING, list what you planted this year that hasn't ripened yet — projects in progress, relationships still forming, healing still happening. **Important:** unfinished doesn't mean failed. Some things take more than one season.
5. Read the HARVESTED column aloud, slowly. Speak after: *"I see what I made. I am proud."*
6. Read the STILL GROWING column aloud. Speak after: *"I trust what isn't done yet. The slow work is still work."*
7. Hold the harvest item to your heart. Speak: *"I carry the proof of this year forward."*
8. Blow out the candle. Keep the harvest item somewhere visible until the winter solstice.

**Affirmation to carry:** *"I am allowed to take inventory of what I've already done."*

**After:** Most of us only ever measure what's left. The equinox forces a measurement of what's *been.* Repeat this same ritual on December 21 to measure the winter harvest.

## WINTER SOLSTICE (~December 21–22) — The Long Night

**What it is:** The sun crosses into Capricorn. Longest night of the year. The light begins to return tomorrow.

**The energy:** Rest, retreat, surrender, faith in the return of the light.

**Ritual: "The Vigil"**
**Tier:** 1 (a single candle, a journal)
**Time needed:** 60 minutes (do this after sundown)

**You'll need:**
- A single candle (any color)
- A journal
- A blanket
- A warm drink (tea, hot chocolate, broth)
- All other lights off — phone in another room

**The ritual:**
1. Wait until full dark. Make the warm drink.
2. Sit in the dark. Wrap up in the blanket. Drink the warm thing.
3. After 5 minutes of just sitting in the dark: light the single candle.
4. Watch the flame for at least 5 minutes. Notice how *small* it is — and how it lights the whole room enough to see by anyway.
5. In your journal, answer these questions slowly:
   - *"What did this year cost me?"*
   - *"What did this year give me?"*
   - *"What did I survive that I haven't fully acknowledged?"*
   - *"What am I quietly hoping for in the year ahead?"*
6. Don't rush the answers. Let the dark hold them. Let the candle witness them.
7. After at least 30 minutes of writing/sitting, blow out the candle. Sit in the dark for one more minute.
8. Speak aloud: *"The light returns tomorrow. I will return with it."*
9. Then turn the lights back on. Go to bed early.

**Affirmation to carry:** *"I trust the dark. I trust the return."*

**After:** The winter solstice is for *being with* what was hard. Not fixing it, not reframing it, not turning it into a lesson — *being with* it. The lesson, if there is one, will come on its own. This is the most important ritual on this entire list. Don't skip it.

# RETROGRADE RITUALS

Retrogrades happen when a planet appears to move backward from Earth's perspective. Each ruling planet has its own retrograde "assignment."

## MERCURY RETROGRADE (~3x per year, 3 weeks each)

**What it is:** Mercury rules communication, technology, travel, and contracts. When it retrogrades: misunderstandings, tech breakdowns, missed flights, exes texting, signed contracts unraveling.

**The reframe:** Mercury retrograde is not bad — it's *recursive.* It asks you to *re*-do, *re*-view, *re*-pair, *re*-flect. The "re-" is the assignment.

**Ritual: "The Three Re-s"**
**Tier:** 0 (no tools)
**Best timing:** First weekend of any Mercury retrograde
**Time needed:** 30 minutes

**The ritual:**
1. Sit with a notebook. Pick three categories from this list:
   - **Re-VIEW** something — a contract, an old project, a relationship, your finances, your health goals
   - **Re-VISIT** someone — a friend you've drifted from, a mentor, a family member you haven't called
   - **Re-PAIR** something — a small broken thing in your life (literally — a button, a screen crack — or metaphorically — an unspoken apology)
   - **Re-FLECT** on something — journal a question you've been avoiding
   - **Re-DO** something — a task that's bothering you, a workout you skipped, a meal you didn't make properly
2. Pick three. Schedule one for each week of the retrograde (Mercury retrogrades are roughly 3 weeks long).
3. Speak aloud: *"This retrograde is not happening to me. It is happening for me. I will use it."*
4. Each week, do the assigned task with intention. Take photos or note the moment when complete.

**Affirmation to carry:** *"I am allowed to look back. Looking back is also moving forward."*

**After:** Most people white-knuckle through Mercury retrograde. People who *use* them come out on the other side with relationships repaired, projects finished, money found, decisions clarified. Be the second kind.

**App opportunity:** Send a notification on the first day of every Mercury retrograde with this ritual.

## VENUS RETROGRADE (~every 18 months, 6 weeks each)

**What it is:** Venus rules love, money, beauty, and values. When it retrogrades: ex-partners reappear, financial reassessment, questioning your style or aesthetic, reevaluating what you value.

**Ritual: "The Values Audit"**
**Tier:** 1 (paper, candle)
**Best timing:** First week of Venus retrograde
**Time needed:** 45 minutes

**You'll need:**
- A piece of paper
- A pen
- A pink, red, or green candle (Venus colors)

**The ritual:**
1. Light the candle. Take 5 deep breaths.
2. On the paper, write three headers: **WHAT I LOVE / WHAT I VALUE / WHAT I FIND BEAUTIFUL.**
3. Under each, list 10 things. Specific, real things. Not "my family" — but "the way my mom laughs when she's not trying to." Not "good food" — but "the bread from that bakery on 8th." Not "art" — but "the painting in my hallway."
4. When done, read all three lists aloud.
5. Now ask yourself, brutally: *"Does my life — how I spend my time, money, attention — actually reflect this list?"*
6. Note where there's a mismatch. Don't fix it tonight. Just *see* it.
7. End: *"I am ready to align with what I actually love."*

**Affirmation to carry:** *"My values are mine to choose. They are also mine to update."*

**After:** Venus retrogrades are for revising your values, not your loved ones. If an ex texts you during a Venus retrograde — they probably will — read this list before you respond. Does answering them align with what you actually love? That's your answer.

## MARS RETROGRADE (~every 2 years, 2.5 months)

**What it is:** Mars rules energy, drive, action, and anger. When it retrogrades: stalled projects, repressed anger surfacing, low energy, frustration with your usual pace.

**Ritual: "The Slow Burn"**
**Tier:** 0
**Best timing:** Throughout Mars retrograde, weekly
**Time needed:** 20 minutes

**The ritual:**
1. Pick one thing you've been pushing forward without success. *(A project that's stalled. A goal that won't budge. A conversation you can't seem to start.)*
2. Sit with it. Out loud, ask: *"What if I stopped pushing for the next month?"*
3. Notice the resistance. Notice the *guilt*. Mars retrograde will surface a lot of "I should be doing more" energy. That guilt is the curriculum.
4. On a piece of paper, write: *"I am not behind. I am exactly where this work needs me right now."*
5. Tape it somewhere you'll see daily.
6. For one week: actively do *less* on that project. Walk away. Read. Rest.
7. At the end of the week, return to it. Notice if anything has shifted in the *quality* of your attention now that the urgency has settled.

**Affirmation to carry:** *"My energy is not endless. Neither does it need to be."*

**After:** Mars retrogrades are notoriously frustrating because every fiber of you wants to push, and pushing isn't working. The assignment is patience. When Mars goes direct again, your energy will return — and you'll know exactly what to use it for.

# ECLIPSE SEASONS (twice a year, ~6 weeks each)

**What they are:** Eclipses come in pairs (one solar, one lunar) every six months, in opposing zodiac signs. They are *not* like regular new/full moons — they are major chapter-closers and chapter-openers. Eclipses are about fated events, sudden shifts, and revelations that arrive whether you're ready or not.

**The rule of eclipses:** Most astrologers recommend **NOT doing manifestation work or major rituals during eclipse season** (the 2-week window around each eclipse). The energy is too unpredictable.

**Instead:** Witness. Receive. Take notes.

**Ritual: "The Eclipse Witness"**
**Tier:** 0
**Best timing:** Day of an eclipse, or the day after
**Time needed:** 20 minutes

**The ritual:**
1. Sit somewhere quiet. Don't try to "do" anything.
2. Open a journal. Write the date and the eclipse details (which sign, solar or lunar — Mapped will provide this).
3. Answer these questions:
   - *"What's been on my mind today, even if it seems random?"*
   - *"What conversation, news, or thought has felt 'loud' this week?"*
   - *"What am I noticing that I usually wouldn't notice?"*
4. Don't try to interpret it. Just record it.
5. Speak aloud: *"I am paying attention. I trust what's revealed."*
6. Close the journal. Open it again **6 months from now** (the next eclipse in the same axis) and re-read.

**Affirmation to carry:** *"What's meant to be revealed will be revealed. My job is to notice."*

**After:** Eclipses don't ask you to act — they ask you to *receive*. The story they're telling becomes clear over the following 6–12 months, often in retrospect. This ritual creates a record so you can connect the dots later.

**App opportunity:** Send a notification on every eclipse with this ritual + the specific astrological context for that eclipse.

# NEW MOON RITUALS BY ZODIAC

Each new moon falls in a specific sign and carries that sign's energy. The intention you set is best calibrated to the sign.

| New Moon Sign | What to Set Intentions Around |
|---|---|
| **Aries** | New starts, courage, taking up space |
| **Taurus** | Money, sensual pleasure, building stability |
| **Gemini** | Communication, learning, social connections |
| **Cancer** | Home, family, emotional safety, nurture |
| **Leo** | Creative projects, visibility, joy |
| **Virgo** | Health routines, work systems, daily habits |
| **Libra** | Relationships, partnership, beauty |
| **Scorpio** | Transformation, intimacy, shadow work |
| **Sagittarius** | Travel, learning, freedom, expansion |
| **Capricorn** | Career, mastery, long-term goals |
| **Aquarius** | Community, innovation, breaking patterns |
| **Pisces** | Spirituality, creativity, dreams, healing |

**Ritual: "The Sign-Specific New Moon"**
**Tier:** 1 (paper, candle, optional crystal at higher tiers)
**Time needed:** 30 minutes

**The ritual:**
1. Check what sign the new moon is in.
2. Use the chart above to pick your intention category.
3. Light a candle. (At Tier 2+, light a candle in the color matching the sign — Aries red, Taurus green, Gemini yellow, etc.)
4. On a piece of paper, write **3 intentions** within that category. Specific, present tense.
5. Read them aloud, three times.
6. Fold the paper. Place it under the candle.
7. Sit with the candle for 10 minutes.
8. Re-read the intentions every morning until the corresponding full moon two weeks later.

**Affirmation to carry:** *"I plant in alignment with the sky."*

**After:** The full moon two weeks later will be in the *opposite* sign (Aries new moon → Libra full moon). That's when you check in on what's shifted. This is the heartbeat of an astrology practice.

# FULL MOON RITUALS BY ZODIAC

Each full moon also falls in a specific sign — opposite to the new moon two weeks before. Full moons illuminate, reveal, and release.

| Full Moon Sign | What to Release / Reflect On |
|---|---|
| **Aries** | Released anger, impulsive choices, where you've been selfish |
| **Taurus** | Stuck-ness, materialism, refusing to change |
| **Gemini** | Gossip, mental scatter, half-truths |
| **Cancer** | Emotional manipulation, codependence, over-mothering |
| **Leo** | Need for validation, ego performance |
| **Virgo** | Perfectionism, over-criticism, anxiety spirals |
| **Libra** | People-pleasing, indecision, false harmony |
| **Scorpio** | Resentment, control, holding the dead too long |
| **Sagittarius** | Dishonesty, escapism, unkept promises |
| **Capricorn** | Workaholism, isolation, joyless duty |
| **Aquarius** | Detachment, intellectual superiority, isolation |
| **Pisces** | Escapism, victimhood, dissolving into others |

**Ritual: "The Sign-Specific Full Moon"**
**Tier:** 1 (paper, candle, fire-safe bowl)
**Time needed:** 30 minutes

**The ritual:**
1. Check what sign the full moon is in.
2. Use the chart above to pick what to release.
3. Light a candle. On paper, write what you're releasing — be specific. Not "my anger," but "the resentment toward my coworker that I've been holding for 4 months."
4. Read it aloud.
5. Burn the paper safely in the bowl.
6. Run the ash under cold water.
7. Pour the wet ash outside or into a houseplant.
8. Speak: *"It's done. I let it go."*

**Affirmation to carry:** *"I release what is illuminated. I keep what is mine."*

**After:** Each full moon is a checkpoint. Two weeks ago you set an intention; now you see what's growing and what needs to die. This is the rhythm.

# SATURN RETURN RITUAL (ages ~28–30 and ~58–60)

**What it is:** Saturn takes ~29.5 years to circle the zodiac. When it returns to where it was when you were born, you experience your "Saturn Return" — a major life recalibration. First one happens late 20s, second happens late 50s.

**The energy:** Brutal honesty about where you are vs. where you thought you'd be. Major life changes (jobs, marriages, moves, breakups, identity shifts).

**Ritual: "The Saturn Audit"**
**Tier:** 1 (paper + candle, ideally over multiple sittings)
**Time needed:** 60 minutes (and probably you'll come back to it)

**You'll need:**
- A journal you'll keep just for this
- A black or dark candle (Saturn's color)
- Time, alone, without interruption

**The ritual:**
1. Light the candle. This is a serious one.
2. In the journal, answer these questions slowly. Take your time. Saturn doesn't reward speed.
   - *"Whose life have I been living? Mine, or someone else's expectations of me?"*
   - *"What have I outgrown but haven't admitted yet?"*
   - *"What am I doing because I 'should' that I no longer want to?"*
   - *"What do I want for the next 30 years that I've never let myself say out loud?"*
3. Don't fix anything tonight. Don't quit your job at 11pm because of a journal entry.
4. Re-read your answers in 30 days. The truth gets clearer over weeks, not minutes.
5. Light the candle again every Sunday during your Saturn Return (which can last 2–3 years total). Each time, ask: *"Am I closer or farther from what's true?"*

**Affirmation to carry:** *"This is the rebuilding. The rebuilding is allowed to be slow."*

**After:** Saturn Returns are not optional. They will happen whether you participate or not. People who *participate* — who tell the truth, make the changes, leave what isn't working — emerge from their Saturn Return as a stronger version of themselves. People who resist tend to repeat the lessons in the next Saturn cycle, much harder.

**App opportunity:** Mapped can identify when a user is in their Saturn Return based on their birth chart and serve this ritual specifically. That's a feature no other ritual app can offer.

# NOTES FOR COWORK

When generating time-of-year rituals, the rules are:

1. **Astrological literacy is mandatory.** Don't reduce eclipses to "intense full moons." Don't tell users to manifest during a Mercury retrograde. Don't confuse a new moon with a dark moon. If Cowork doesn't know the difference, it shouldn't generate the ritual.

2. **The four solar thresholds are non-negotiable annual content.** Spring equinox, summer solstice, autumn equinox, winter solstice. These are the spine of the year. Plan content for each one weeks in advance — they're predictable.

3. **Retrogrades have specific assignments per planet:**
   - Mercury → re-view, re-do, re-pair
   - Venus → re-evaluate values
   - Mars → rest, redirect energy
   - Jupiter → re-define your beliefs
   - Saturn → audit your structures
   - Uranus → re-imagine the future
   - Neptune → distinguish dreams from delusions
   - Pluto → confront what's dying

4. **Eclipse rituals should never include manifestation.** Eclipse energy is too unpredictable. The assignment is *witness*, not *create*.

5. **Push notifications are gold here.** Mapped already has the user's chart. The app knows when an eclipse hits, when a planet stations retrograde, when a new moon lands in their 7th house. Send a notification with the exact ritual for that day. *That* is what makes Mapped sticky.

6. **The app should always provide context first.** Before the ritual: "Today is the autumn equinox. Day and night are equal. Here's what your sign is doing in this moment." Then the ritual. The context is what makes the ritual feel personalized instead of generic.
