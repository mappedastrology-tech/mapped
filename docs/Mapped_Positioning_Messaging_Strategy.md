# Mapped — Positioning, Messaging & Copy Strategy

**Version 1.0 · July 2026 · Owner: Taylor**

This is the master reference for how Mapped talks — on the website, in the app, in the
App Store, in notifications, and everywhere else. It exists to do two jobs:

1. **Govern** every piece of copy we write.
2. **Audit** every piece of copy that already exists.

> **How to use this doc as an audit tool.** Hand this file to Claude (or any editor) with a
> screen, a page, or a string of copy and say: *"Audit this against the Mapped positioning

> **Copy Audit Rubric (§10)** — then flag violations and rewrite. If a line can't be traced

## 1. The one-page summary

**What Mapped is:** an all-in-one astrology and esoterica app that meets you exactly where
you stand — whether you're a skeptic who likes the vibe or a practicing witch — and grows
with you.

**The core idea:** *Astrology for your actual life.* Grounded, specific, useful. A lens for
understanding yourself and your timing, never a prediction that tells you what to do.

**The wedge no competitor has:** two dials the user controls.

- **The belief dial (Tone):** Grounded → Balanced → Mystical. The same insight, in the
  language you trust — nervous-system talk or lunar cycles, your call.
- **The tooling dial (Tier):** Just Me → Household → Light Spiritual → Full Practice → Witchy.
  Rituals scaled to what you actually own and believe, from breathwork to full spellwork.

**Why it wins:** every other serious app forces one register on you. Co-Star is cold and
blunt. CHANI is beautiful but assumes you already believe. Nebula and Sanctuary push
psychic upsells. Mapped is the only one that lets *you* set the temperature — and backs it
with real depth (full natal chart, transits, astrocartography, a Duolingo-style learning
library) and a trust-first, anti-manipulation voice.

**One line for the world:** *Mapped — astrology for your actual life.*

**One line for the skeptic:** *You don't have to believe in it for it to be useful.*

**One line for the practitioner:** *Finally, an app deep enough to keep up with you.*

## 2. The market & the white space

The astrology-app market is large and growing (multi-billion-dollar category, ~6–20%+ CAGR
across estimates), and it skews young and female — roughly 60% Gen Z, with ~43% of women
18–49 saying they believe in astrology. Crucially, **belief is not required for engagement**:
a huge segment uses these apps casually, for meaning and self-reflection, without treating
them as a decision engine. That casual-but-curious majority is Mapped's biggest opportunity —
and the group every competitor either ignores or alienates.

**Where the competitors sit — and where they leave room:**

| App | Positioning | Strength | The gap it leaves Mapped |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Co-Star** | Blunt, minimalist, "hyper-personalized" | Cultural cachet, social/friends layer | Cold, sometimes anxiety-inducing, cryptic; growing paywall backlash. No warmth, no depth, no growth path. |
| **CHANI** | Human-authored, wellness, queer-feminist | Gorgeous writing, values, rituals | Assumes you already believe; fully mystical register; premium price; can't scale to skeptics. |
| **The Pattern** | Psychological self-discovery | Uncanny personality/timing insight | Narrow (mostly personality + bonds); no learning, tools, or belief flexibility. |
| **Nebula** | All-in-one + psychic marketplace | Feature breadth | Upsell-heavy, opaque pricing, psychic-hotline energy; shallow depth. |
| **Sanctuary** | Live astrologer chat | Real humans on demand | Expensive, transactional; not a daily companion. |

**The white space Mapped owns:** *the only app that adjusts to your level of belief and
practice.* One product that a data-driven skeptic and a devoted tarot reader can both call
theirs — held together by genuine depth and a voice that never manipulates. Nobody else is
standing here.

## 3. Audience — the spectrum, not a persona

Mapped's positioning hero is the **spectrum itself**. We don't pick one user; we make the
range legible and let people self-locate. Four reference points along the dial:

**A. The Curious Skeptic ("I don't really believe in this, but…")**
Likes the aesthetic, the self-reflection, the ritual of checking in. Repelled by woo,
absolutes, and being told what to do. *Mapped for them:* the Grounded tone, "a lens not a
prediction," science-aware framing, the natal chart as a self-knowledge tool. **This is our
top-of-funnel and our biggest untapped segment.**

**B. The Casual Believer ("It's kind of scary accurate")**
The default astrology-app user. Reads their horoscope, knows their big three, dabbles.
*Mapped for them:* the Balanced tone (default), daily guidance, Maps, Dolly, the "collectible"
learning tiles.

**C. The Growing Practitioner ("I want to go deeper")**
Buying crystals, pulling tarot, learning the houses. Wants an app that respects and rewards
depth. *Mapped for them:* the learning library, the tier system climbing toward Full Practice,
transits, human design, numerology.

**D. The Devoted Witch ("This is my practice")**
Runs rituals, works with correspondences, reads for others. Usually underserved by mass apps.
*Mapped for them:* Tier 4 (Witchy), Mystical tone, spellwork language, the depth of the
correspondence system.

**The strategic move:** never make one group feel the app "isn't for them." Copy should
signal *"wherever you are on this, we've got a setting for you."* The tier/tone dials are how
we say that in the product; the messaging must say it in words.

> voice that treats you as capable — a lens for your life, never a prediction.

**Category we're claiming:** *grounded, all-in-one astrology* — or, more sharply,
*astrology you can adjust.*

**The enemy we position against:** not other apps by name, but three things —
**cold/cryptic** (Co-Star), **woo-that-assumes-belief** (the mystical mass market), and
**manipulative/anxiety-farming** (paywalls, doom-notifications, psychic upsells). Mapped is
warm, adjustable, and honest.

## 5. Messaging pillars

Every headline, feature description, and CTA should trace to one of these four. If it doesn't,
cut it or rewrite it.

**Pillar 1 — Meets you where you are.**
*The dials. The whole spectrum in one app.*
Proof: Tone (Grounded/Balanced/Mystical), Tier (Just Me → Witchy), warm onboarding, no
gatekeeping.
Sample line: *"Set the dial from grounded to mystical. Same you, your language."*

**Pillar 2 — For your actual life.**
*Specific, useful, grounded. Not generic horoscope memes.*
Proof: rituals with real steps and timing, chart readings tied to real life stages,
relationship/career context in onboarding, "no vague."
Sample line: *"Real astrology, tied to the week you're actually having."*

**Pillar 3 — Depth you can grow into.**
*A whole world: chart, transits, Maps, tarot, rituals, numerology, human design, palmistry,
and a learning library that teaches you.*
Proof: 700+ reference entries, Duolingo-style courses, full natal chart, astrocartography.
Sample line: *"Start with your sun sign. End up reading your own transits."*

**Pillar 4 — A lens, not a prediction.**
*Agency-first. Honest. Never manipulative.*
Proof: "you always have agency," rest-day forgiveness, no doom-notifications, evidence-first
learning with honest safety notes, no social leaderboard.
Sample line: *"We'll never tell you what's going to happen. We'll help you understand what's
going on."*

## 6. Voice & tone rules

**Brand voice in five words:** *grounded, specific, knowledgeable, warm, adjustable.*

Mapped's product already ships a full tone system for ritual content (see
`Mapped_Cowork_Master_Prompt.md`). That system governs *in-app generated content*. This
section governs **marketing and UI copy** — the website, App Store, onboarding, buttons,
empty states, notifications.

**We are:**
- **Literate, not breathy.** Editorial, like a beautifully-printed almanac. Confident sentences.
- **Specific over sweeping.** "Your Saturn return is a three-year renovation, not a curse,"
  not "big changes are coming."
- **Warm, not saccharine.** Kind and human. Never infantilizing, never "beautiful soul."
- **Plain-spoken about belief.** We name the skepticism in the room and make it welcome.
- **Calm about money.** No fake scarcity, no countdown-timer pressure, no doom.

**We are not:**
- Cold or cryptic for its own sake (that's Co-Star's lane).
- Preachy or fully-woo by default (that assumes belief we haven't earned).
- Hype-y or manipulative (no "manifest your dream life," no "limited spots").

### Banned language (marketing + UI)

Inherits the ritual banned-list and extends it. **Never use:**

- Pet names: "darling," "beloved," "beautiful soul," "sacred sister," "cosmic queen," "bestie."
- Woo clichés: "vibrate higher," "trust the process," "the universe has your back," "tap into
  your highest self," "manifest your dream life," "raise your vibration," "divine timing."
- Fear/urgency sales: "don't miss out," "limited time," "spots filling fast," "last chance,"
  anything with a fake countdown.
- Absolutes & prediction: "this will happen," "you will meet," "guaranteed," "your destiny is."
- Emoji clusters: "🌙✨", "✨🔮✨". (Occasional single glyph in product UI is fine; never in
  clusters, never as a substitute for a real sentence.)
- Overpromising outcomes: "change your life," "find your soulmate," "unlock abundance."

### Punctuation & style
- Sentence case for most headings; the wordmark is **mapped.** (lowercase, with the period).
- Em dashes and periods over exclamation points. Rarely, if ever, an exclamation point.
- Contractions are good. Second person ("you") is the default.
- One idea per sentence. Cut every word you can and still make the point.

## 7. Vocabulary — say this, not that

| Say | Not |
|---|---|
| a lens | a prediction / your fate |
| your chart / your placements | your destiny |
| timing / what's going on | what's going to happen |
| understand | discover the secret |
| practice | spell (except in Tier 4 / Mystical) |
| grounded | scientific (don't overclaim) |
| guidance | answers |
| notes / a read | a warning |
| Dolly, your guide | psychic / oracle (Dolly is a guide, not a fortune teller) |
| set the tone / pick your tier | choose your vibe |
| the week you're having | your future |

**Naming conventions (per UX audit — resolve the nav collisions):**
- **You / Chart** — your natal chart + identity.
- **Home** — daily guidance.
- **Maps** — relationships / astrocartography (connections).
- **Dolly** — the AI guide.
- **Almanac** — the sky right now.
- **Rituals** — moon work & practice (rename the old bottom-tab "Practice" to this).
- **Learn** — the course library.
- **Library** — the reference lookup.
- **Journal** — reflection.
- Keep one word = one place. Never use "Learn" or "Practice" for two different destinations.

## 10. Copy audit rubric

Run any piece of copy against this. A line ships only if it's **yes** on all of 1–7.

1. **Traceable.** Does it clearly express one of the four pillars (§5)? If you can't name the
   pillar, rewrite.
2. **A lens, not a prediction.** Does it avoid promising outcomes, predicting events, or issuing
   warnings? (No "you will," "guaranteed," "your destiny.")
3. **Adjustable-aware.** For anything about how Mapped speaks or what it asks of the user, does
   it respect the tone/tier dials rather than assuming one register of belief?
4. **Specific, not vague.** Could this sentence appear in a generic horoscope app? If yes, make
   it specific to Mapped or to the actual chart/timing.
5. **Voice-clean.** No banned language (§6). No pet names, no woo clichés, no urgency/fear
   sales, no emoji clusters, no exclamation-point hype.
6. **Warm & agency-first.** Does it treat the reader as capable and leave them in control? No
   infantilizing, no manipulation, no anxiety-farming.
7. **Vocabulary-correct.** Uses the "say this, not that" list (§7) and the resolved
   nav/naming conventions (one word = one place).

**Scoring shorthand for an audit pass:**
- 🟢 Ships as-is.
- 🟡 Right idea, wrong words — rewrite to fix a specific rubric line.
- 🔴 Off-strategy (predicts, manipulates, assumes belief, or is generic) — replace.

**When auditing a full screen, also check:**
- Does the heading match a real pillar, or is it decoration?
- Is there exactly one clear primary action?
- Do empty/error/edge states exist and follow the calm, human patterns in §8.9–8.10?
- Is any nav word used for two destinations? (Fix per §7.)

## 11. Do / Don't cheat sheet

**Do**
- Name the skepticism and make it welcome.
- Be specific about the chart and the timing.
- Keep money talk calm and honest.
- Use the dials as the signature story.
- Write like an editor, not a mystic-for-hire.

**Don't**
- Predict, warn, or promise outcomes.
- Assume the reader already believes.
- Use pet names, woo clichés, or urgency/fear.
- Overload nav words or invent new ones.
- Let a headline be pretty but meaningless.
