# Mapped — Learn Section Image List

Every image the Learn section still needs, as a flat checklist. **115 files.**
Filenames are exact — they are the app's real entry ids, so a correctly-named
file drops in with no renaming. Counts were read out of the code today: **812
reference entries, 90 with art (78 tarot + 12 zodiac), 722 without.**

The reasoning behind each rule lives in `Mapped_Art_Brief_Library.md`. This is
the list to work from.

---

## The art style — paste this into every prompt

> Painted plate in the Mapped house style: oil-on-paper brushwork, warm natural
> light from the upper left, soft feathered edges, painted on a pure white
> ground. One subject, centred, no ground plane, no cast shadow, no border, no
> text or labels of any kind. Natural colour for the subject — no forced
> palette. No faces and no human figures.

Attach **The Anvil** as the style anchor, and include this line with it every
time:

> The reference image is for PAINT STYLE ONLY — brushwork, lighting, edge
> quality, white ground. Do not include an anvil, a blacksmith's tool, or any
> part of the reference's subject. The only subject is the one described below.

Seven plates came back with an anvil painted into them when that line was left
out. It is not optional.

**Delivery:** 1024 × 1024 PNG, square, flat white background — do **not**
pre-cut them. I key the white out with the app's own algorithm so every plate
matches. Fresh chat every 8–12 images, re-attach the anchor each time.

**The size test:** these render at **31 × 31 px** in search results and 60px in
the detail sheet. Squint at your 1024 — if you can't tell what it is, it fails.
One clear silhouette, strong value contrast, no fine detail.

## Deliberately not on this list

- **Meditation entries (43)** — they're techniques, not things. The honest
  illustration of "body scan" is a person doing it, which the house style
  forbids. That's a design question, not a drawing task.
- **The 5 chakra concept entries** — `chakra-subtle-body`, `chakra-nadis`,
  `chakra-prana`, `chakra-kundalini`, `chakra-bija-mantra`. A yantra would
  misrepresent them and there's no obvious object for "prana".
- **The 15 non-countable numerology entries** — karmic debts, chart positions,
  angel numbers. The counting trick breaks at 111.
- **The open-ended domains** — crystals 151, herbs 127, dreams 93, oils 76.
  Worth doing in tranches later (§6 of the full brief); the Learn section stops
  looking unfinished at 104 without them.
- **Anything at 20px.** The chart wheel's sign ring keeps its line-art SVG.
  Painted plates are mud at that size.

## Sending them back

One flat folder per batch, PNGs named **exactly** as listed. The filenames are
the only thing that has to be perfect — they're the entry ids and they become
the image paths verbatim.

When a batch lands I key the white out, resize, install, add the `image:` field
to that domain's reference factory, then QA every plate against this list and
report anything off before it ships.
